  not orphans; `local-bin` intentionally installs every regular file in `bin/` and `scripts/`.

  `files/vale/styles/Google/**` (upstream) are not cleanup candidates.
- `files/gui/hypr/hyprlock/**` + `hyprlock.conf` are kept on purpose: `hyprland/files.nix` says to
  restore them together with the hyprlock package.
- `zapret2`, `hyprwhspr`, `llama-cpp-qwen4exp-rocm`, `game`, `flow`, `wl`, `flake`,
  `scratchpad-geometry` all have live consumers and were left alone; the package audit's unverified
  tail (`game`, `wl`, `target`, `flake`, `flow`, `scratchpad-geometry`) was resolved by hand.
