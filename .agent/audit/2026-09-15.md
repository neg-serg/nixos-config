## Findings raised during the fixes

- **N1 (medium, RESOLVED — upstream by design, no repo fix) · forcing the whole user-unit set
  fails** — `nix eval .#nixosConfigurations.odin.config.systemd.user.services` aborts with
  `The option 'systemd.user.services.adw-bluetooth-daemon.startLimitBurst' was accessed but has no value defined`.
  Root cause, checked in nixpkgs: `nixos/lib/systemd-unit-options.nix:288` declares
  `startLimitBurst`/`startLimitIntervalSec` with `mkOption` and **no default**, and
  `nixos/lib/systemd-lib.nix:611` itself guards their use with `options.<...>.isDefined`. So forcing
  every attribute of every unit fails *by design* (the unit comes from nixpkgs' own
  `services.adw-bluetooth` module, used at modules/hardware/config.nix:55). Inspect defined options
  (`options.<name>.isDefined`) instead of dumping the whole tree.
- **N2 (medium) · the generated global ripgrep config hid tracked files** — the
  `--glob=!package-lock.json` line in `modules/user/nix-maid/cli/search.nix` (rendered to
  `~/.config/ripgrep/ripgreprc`) hides the three tracked `package-lock.json` files from rg/fd; fixed
  together with B2.
- **N3 (low, RESOLVED with C6) · dead helper surface** — `mkSimpleService`, `mkSimpleTimer` and
  `mkSimpleSocket` in `lib/systemd-user.nix` had no call sites; only `mkUserOneshot` and
  `mkUnitFromPresets` were used. Dropped in `d75f8fd0e` and replaced by the module-shaped
  `mkUserService` (the shape every real unit in the repo actually uses).
